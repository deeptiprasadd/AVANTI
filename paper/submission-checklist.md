# Submission checklist: International Journal on Digital Libraries

Journal page: https://link.springer.com/journal/799

## What I could and could not check (2026-11-04)

Springer's journal pages (aims and scope, submission guidelines) redirect to a
cookie sign-in and could not be read automatically. Open these in a browser:

- https://link.springer.com/journal/799/aims-and-scope
- https://link.springer.com/journal/799/submission-guidelines

Read from search results only, not from the scope page: the journal covers the
theory and practice of digital information across its lifecycle, with listed
topics including information access and retrieval and AI for digital
libraries, and welcomes links with information retrieval, artificial
intelligence and library and information science.

Not known, to read on the submission-guidelines page: article types and
length limits, whether system or tool papers are accepted as regular papers,
the LaTeX template (Springer Nature journals commonly provide one), open
access options and fees, peer-review model, and journal-specific rules.

## Policy on automated tools (read directly)

Source: https://group.springernature.com/gp/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-researchers-editors-reviewers

- Software tools cannot be authors; authors remain accountable for the work.
- Use of such tools in the research must be declared, with tool names and
  versions, dates and the instructions used. The page itself gives two
  locations (Methods or equivalent; Introduction or Acknowledgements) and
  contradicts itself on copy editing, so confirm with the journal.
- Results produced with a tool must be validated by the authors.

What this means for this paper: Section 6.1 and the Declarations in
`paper/draft.md` contain REQUIRED placeholders for how the row-by-row
judgements were produced. They must be completed accurately before
submission. Omitting a method step that the policy asks to be declared is a
breach of it, whatever the project's other documents say.

## Before submission

- [ ] Read the aims and scope and submission guidelines in a browser; adjust
      the draft to the article type and length.
- [ ] Complete the disclosure placeholders (Section 6.1, Declarations).
- [ ] Confirm every author list in `paper/references.md`; write the literature
      review in Section 2 and add the missing references.
- [ ] Decide whether to fix the reference parser first (see TODO.md, D2). If
      it is fixed, a third unseen set is needed for an honest number.
- [ ] Put the repository URL and an archived release (Zenodo or similar) in
      the data and code statement; confirm the repository is public.
- [ ] Convert to the journal's template; make figures (a diagram of the
      pipeline, a plot of Table 1).
- [ ] Funding, competing interests, author contributions, ethics (none
      expected: no human subjects).
- [ ] Cover letter: why this journal, one paragraph on the contribution, and
      the disclosure statement if the policy asks for it there.
